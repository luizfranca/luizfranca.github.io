## Hardware
### Main
- **Computer:** [MacBook Pro (14-inch, 2023)](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111340){:target="_blank"}, 2023, M2 Pro, 32GB RAM, and 1TB SSD. This is the computer I use most of the time.
- **Mouse:** [Magic Mouse](https://www.apple.com/ca/shop/product/mxk63am/a/magic-mouse-usb%E2%80%91c-black-multi-touch-surface){:target="_blank"}. I'm too used to using the gestures on the magic mouse to switch to a "regular" one now. I do think sometimes about getting a magic trackpad for the gestures and using a regular mouse like the [Logitech Lift Vertical](https://www.canadacomputers.com/en/ergonomic-mice/219389/logitech-lift-vertical-ergonomic-wireless-mouse-graphite-910-006466.html){:target="_blank"} I use at work. 
- **Keyboard:**  [Keychron Q1 Max](https://www.keychron.com/products/keychron-q1-max-qmk-via-wireless-custom-mechanical-keyboard?_pos=1&_sid=fee989094&_ss=r){:target="_blank"} purple with Gateron Jupiter Red Switches and ANSI Layout. Using the knob is as pleasing as you'd expect.
- **Displays:** Two [DELL 27 4K UHD S2721QS](https://www.dell.com/en-ca/shop/dell-27-4k-uhd-monitor-s2721qs/apd/210-axlg/monitors-monitor-accessories){:target="_blank"} with a 60hz refresh rate. I've never been able to see the difference between 60hz and higher refresh rates anyways. I use my mac on clamshell mode. Two screens are enough for me.
- **Speakers:**: I alternate between the three below, but mostly use the Logitech ones.
  - [Logitech Z207](https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B074KJ6JQW){:target="_blank"}: They are ok. The bluetooth connection is a bit laggy when I start playing something new, but after it starts playing it's fine. I don't know if it's a software issue or not but this happened on both of my computers, so I generally use it wired with my Mac.
  - [Sony WH-1000XM5](https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B0BXYCS74H){:target="_blank"}: I like the sound and noise cancelling but the smaller band than the M4s gives me a headache sometimes, also it doesn't fold.
  - [AirPods Pro 2](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111851){:target="_blank"}: My everyday earphones.
- **Microphone:** [Razer Seiren V3 Mini](https://www.razer.com/ca-en/streaming-microphones/razer-seiren-v3-mini){:target="_blank"}. I don't understand anything about audio, but they work fine. I like that it comes with a desk stand base. I don't have space on my table to mount an arm for the mic.
- **Dock Station:** [CalDigit TS3 Plus](https://www.caldigit.com/ts3-plus/){:target="_blank"}. I love not having to plug a million cable everytime I want to plug/unplug my Mac. It's the DisplayPort is a bit finicky, I wish I had the model with two Thunderbolt ports.

## Software
### Main
- **OS:** macOS 26.5.2 (25F84). I try to keep on the latest unless I see it's unstable.
- **Terminal:** iTerm 2.
- **Shell:** Zsh with Oh My Zsh and Powerlevel10k.
- **Editor:** VS Code.
- **Broswer:** Firefox

### Gaming PC
- **OS:** Windows 10 Pro (22H2). I refuse to upgrade to 11 or higher with the keylogger that is Recall and AI slop all over it. I only use Windows at all because of games compatibility.
- **Broswer:** Firefox
